Privacy Policy

Effective date: · Document version: 2.1

Short version: this app does not use your data for analytics, advertising, or profiling, and does not sell or share personal information with third parties. The app only processes what is needed to work on your device.

1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how this application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you install and use the App on your mobile device. By tapping "Agree & Continue", you confirm that you have read and understood this policy and that you consent to the practices described below.

If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not continue to use the App and uninstall it from your device.

2 Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be explicit about the boundaries of our data practices:

  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, usage statistics, or behavioral event data.
  • We do not use your information for profiling, personalization, or advertising.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share personal information with third parties.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or cross-app tracking technologies.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera unless you explicitly grant permission for a specific feature, and any such access stays on your device.

3 Information Processed on Your Device

The App may store settings, preferences, and locally created content on your device so that the App can function as expected. This information is kept in the App's private storage area on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed when you delete the App or clear its data.

A technical device fingerprint (a non-reversible hash generated locally from rendering characteristics of your device) may be computed on-device solely to support basic integrity and abuse-prevention checks. This value is not linked to your identity, is not used for analytics or advertising, and is never shared with third parties for their own purposes.

4 Network Requests

To keep this privacy notice up to date, the App may make a limited network request to our configuration service. That request contains only the App identifier and the on-device device fingerprint described above. It does not contain your name, email address, location, contacts, or content. The response is used solely to determine whether a newer version of this privacy notice should be displayed.

If an updated privacy notice is available, you may be redirected to it before continuing. Otherwise, the local copy of this notice is shown. No advertising or analytics SDKs are involved.

5 Legal Bases and Purpose Limitation

Where data protection laws require a legal basis, we rely on your consent (provided when you accept this policy) and on our legitimate interest in operating a safe, functional App. Any processing is limited to what is strictly necessary to deliver the App's core functionality and to maintain the integrity of the service.

6 Data Retention

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no server-side retention period for personal information. On-device data remains until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

7 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Since we do not maintain a server-side profile of you, most requests can be satisfied directly on your device by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

You may contact us using the details in Section 10 for any privacy-related question or request.

8 Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the updated version will be presented to you for review before you continue using the App. Your continued use after acceptance of an updated policy constitutes your agreement to the revised terms.
